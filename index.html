Three everyday jobs. One simple flow.

Run the day, not the admin.

The current sample-data alpha demonstrates these three workflows. It is not yet a hosted multi-business system.

01

Book with confidence.

See places left across the week. The demo stops over-capacity bookings and bookings with expired vaccinations, and suggests another day when full.

02

Know who's in.

Check today's dogs, spot approaching or expired vaccination dates, and add Ate, Walked, Played or Napped updates in one tap.

03

Share a little Pupdate.

Preview one dog's daily notes in an owner-style view, without displaying other dogs' details in that view.
